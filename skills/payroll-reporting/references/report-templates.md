# Payroll Report Templates

This document contains the standard structure and content for each report type. Use these as templates when generating Word documents.

---

## Executive Summary Template

**Purpose:** High-level payroll health and strategic insights for C-suite and board-level stakeholders

**Structure:**

### 1. Title Page
```
[Company Logo]
PAYROLL EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Period: [Month/Quarter Year]
Date Generated: [Date]
CONFIDENTIAL - FOR INTERNAL USE ONLY
```

### 2. Executive Summary (1/2 page)
Brief paragraph addressing:
- Overall payroll health status (stable, concerning, growing, etc.)
- Key strategic finding or concern
- Bottom-line impact (total cost, headcount, variance vs. budget)

Example: "Payroll processed successfully for [Month] with total gross pay of $2.4M supporting 325 active FTE. Year-to-date, we remain on budget with a favorable 0.8% variance. One notable area requiring attention: overtime has increased to 8.2% of regular pay, up from historical average of 5.5%, suggesting either understaffing or unplanned operational need in Operations."

### 3. KPI Dashboard (1/2 page)
Table format with 4-6 key metrics, showing Current Period | Prior Period | Variance | Status

Key metrics for executives:
- **Total Gross Pay:** Current period amount
- **Active Headcount:** Current active FTE count
- **Net Pay:** Total net disbursement
- **Employer Cost (FICA, benefit contribution):** Total burden
- **Cost per FTE:** [Total Cost / FTE count]
- **Payroll Processing Status:** On-time? Any exceptions?

Visual indicator for each (Green/Yellow/Red based on variance to budget or prior period):
- Green: Within expected range
- Yellow: Slight variance (±5%), requires monitoring
- Red: Significant variance or concern area

### 4. Period Highlights (3-5 key observations)
Short bullet-point section. Each highlight should:
- State the fact
- Provide context
- Indicate if action is needed

Example bullets:
- "Headcount increased by 8 FTE net (5 new hires, 1 termination), bringing active roster to 325. Growth aligns with Q1 hiring plan."
- "Overtime jumped to 8.2% of regular pay (was 5.5% prior quarter). Operations team reports temporary customer spike; expected to normalize in Q2."
- "Benefit enrollment in new medical plan reached 94% participation. Finance team working to reconcile 6% non-elected employees."

### 5. Cost Analysis by Department (table + narrative)
Table: Department | Headcount | Total Cost | Cost/FTE | vs. Prior Period

Narrative: 2-3 sentences calling out largest departments, significant changes, any concerns.

### 6. Risk & Action Items (bullet format)
Identify any:
- Data quality concerns
- Payroll processing exceptions or delays
- Unusual patterns in deductions, taxes, or costs
- Compliance or control issues
- Staffing or cost forecast implications

Format: "Risk/Item | Severity | Mitigation/Action | Owner | Target Date"

### 7. Appendix: Detailed Reference Tables
- Full department breakdown table
- Cost center detail (if relevant)
- Tax withholding summary
- Benefits deduction summary by type
- Pay area breakdown (if multi-state/country)

## HR Operations Report Template

**Purpose:** Detailed workforce tracking and people-focused metrics for HR operations team

**Structure:**

### 1. Title Page
```
[Company Logo]
HR PAYROLL OPERATIONS REPORT
Period: [Month/Year]
Date Generated: [Date]
INTERNAL DOCUMENT
```

### 2. Workforce Summary (1/2 page)
- **Active FTE:** Count of current active employees
- **FTE vs. Budget/Headcount Target:** Show variance
- **Contingent/Contract Workers:** If applicable, separate count
- **Overall Status:** Staffed adequately? Any hiring/attrition concerns?

Table format:
| Metric | Current Period | Prior Period | Variance |
|--------|---|---|---|
| Active FTE | X | X | X |
| Headcount | X | X | X |
| Contingent Workers | X | X | X |

### 3. Movement Report (1 page)
**New Hires** table:
- Employee count added this period
- Department/location breakdown
- Names, hire dates, departments, salaries (summary of top earners)
- Average salary of new hires vs. department average
- Total salary impact on payroll

**Terminations** table:
- Voluntary vs. involuntary count
- Department breakdown
- Severance impact (if applicable)
- Reason categories (resignation, retirement, termination for cause)
- Net salary savings from terminations

**Transfers/Promotions** table:
- Internal movement count
- Salary impact (increases, no change, lateral)
- Department-to-department flow

Narrative commentary:
- Overall movement activity (high/low compared to historical)
- Attrition rate implications
- Any concerning patterns
- Impact on payroll from new hire onboarding or termination processing

### 4. Department Breakdown (table + brief narrative)
Table format with all departments:
| Department | Headcount | FTE | Avg. Salary | Total Cost | Prior Period Cost | Variance |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations | X | X | X | X | X | X% |
| Customer Success | X | X | X | X | X | X% |
| Finance | X | X | X | X | X | X% |
| HR | X | X | X | X | X | X% |
| Executive | X | X | X | X | X | X% |

Narrative: 2-3 sentences on largest departments, notable changes, staffing adequacy.

### 5. Benefits Enrollment & Deductions (1/2 page)
Summary table:
| Benefit Type | Participants | Participation % | Total Deduction | Avg. per Employee |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Medical Insurance | X | X% | $X | $X |
| Dental Insurance | X | X% | $X | $X |
| 401(k) | X | X% | $X | $X |
| FSA/HSA | X | X% | $X | $X |

Narrative:
- Enrollment changes from prior period
- Participation rates vs. historical
- Any employees with coverage gaps
- Cost trends
- Action items (e.g., "Follow-up needed with 6 employees who have not made medical elections")

### 6. Payroll Processing & Compliance (1/2 page)
- **Processing Timeline:** Days from period close to payroll validation
- **Exceptions/Corrections:** Count and nature of corrections made
- **Approval Chain:** Sign-off status, any delays
- **Data Quality:** Missing or anomalous data identified
- **Compliance Items:** Time-off tracking, wage/hour compliance, benefit compliance

Action items specific to HR operations and compliance.

### 7. Upcoming Actions/Deadlines
- Benefit plan changes
- Enrollment periods opening/closing
- Payroll system updates or cutoff dates
- Regulatory deadlines (tax filings, reporting)
- Staff transitions pending

## Finance Report Template

**Purpose:** Cost accountability and financial reconciliation for finance/accounting teams

**Structure:**

### 1. Title Page
```
[Company Logo]
PAYROLL FINANCE REPORT
Period: [Month/Year]
Fiscal Period: [FP date range]
Date Generated: [Date]
CONFIDENTIAL - FINANCE TEAM
```

### 2. P&L Impact Summary (1/2 page)
- **Total Gross Payroll:** Current period, prior period, YTD
- **Employer Costs (FICA, benefit contributions):** Amount and % of gross
- **Total Payroll Expense:** Gross + employer cost
- **% of Revenue (if available):** Payroll as % of company revenue
- **Variance to Budget:** Amount and %

Narrative: Assess whether payroll is tracking to budget and forecast. Highlight any material variances.

### 3. Cost Center Allocation (1 page)
Table showing cost distribution across GL accounts:
| Cost Center | Department | Headcount | Total Payroll Cost | Prior Period | Variance | GL Account |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CC-001 | Operations | X | $X | $X | X% | 6100 |
| CC-002 | Customer Success | X | $X | $X | X% | 6100 |
| CC-003 | Finance | X | $X | $X | X% | 6100 |
| CC-004 | Executive | X | $X | $X | X% | 6100 |

Narrative: Largest cost centers, variance drivers, any allocation issues or corrections needed.

### 4. Gross Pay Analysis (1/2 page)
Breakdown by wage type:
| Wage Component | Current Period | Prior Period | YTD | % of Total |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Regular Pay | $X | $X | $X | X% |
| Overtime | $X | $X | $X | X% |
| Bonus/Variable | $X | $X | $X | X% |
| Other (commissions, etc.) | $X | $X | $X | X% |

Commentary: Trends in variable pay, expected year-end forecasts, budget implications.

### 5. Tax Liability Summary (1/2 page)
Table:
| Tax Type | Current Period | Employer Liability | Employee Withholding | Total |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Federal Income Tax | - | - | $X | $X |
| State Income Tax (by state) | - | - | $X | $X |
| FICA-Social Security (6.2%) | $X | - | $X | $X |
| FICA-Medicare (1.45%) | $X | - | $X | $X |
| SUI/SDI (by state) | $X | - | - | $X |

Narrative:
- Total tax liability
- Tax accrual vs. actual deposit (if reconciliation data available)
- Any multi-state tax complexities or adjustments
- Projected annual tax burden

### 6. Accrual vs. Actual Reconciliation (1/2 page)
If accrual data is available:
| Item | Accrued (Prior Period) | Current Actual | Variance | Explanation |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Gross Pay | $X | $X | $X | [Variance explanation] |
| FICA Withholding | $X | $X | $X | |
| Tax Liability | $X | $X | $X | |

Narrative: Overall accrual accuracy, any material exceptions, implications for financial statements.

### 7. GL Posting Summary (1/2 page)
Journal entries posted this period:
| GL Account | Account Description | Amount | Posted Date | Status |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| 6100 | Salaries & Wages | $X | [Date] | Posted |
| 2100 | FICA Payable | $X | [Date] | Posted |
| 2150 | Federal Tax Payable | $X | [Date] | Posted |

Narrative: All postings completed on-time? Any held items? Any reconciliation issues with payroll and GL?

### 8. Deduction & Benefit Cost Analysis (1/2 page)
- Total benefit costs (company contribution + employee deduction)
- Trends vs. prior periods and budget
- Per-employee cost by benefit type
- Any enrollment or cost changes impacting payroll

### 9. Action Items & Recommendations
Finance-specific actions:
- Accrual adjustments needed
- GL account reviews or reconciliation items
- Tax filing deadlines and prep
- Annual payroll tax planning (W-2, FICA reconciliation, etc.)
- Budget forecast implications

## Audit Report Template

**Purpose:** Control evidence and compliance documentation for internal/external audit

**Structure:**

### 1. Title Page
```
[Company Logo]
PAYROLL CONTROL & AUDIT REPORT
Period: [Month/Year]
Date Generated: [Date]
PREPARED FOR AUDIT TEAM
CONFIDENTIAL
```

### 2. Processing Timeline & Control Execution (1 page)
Document the payroll processing flow with dates and approvals:

| Step | Expected Date | Actual Date | Owner | Status | Notes |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data Import/Validation | [Date] | [Date] | Payroll Mgr | Completed | All validations passed |
| Calculation & Testing | [Date] | [Date] | Payroll Analyst | Completed | No exceptions |
| Manager Review & Approval | [Date] | [Date] | Dept Mgrs | Completed | 5/5 managers signed off |
| Finance Review | [Date] | [Date] | Controller | Completed | No adjustment requests |
| Execution & Posting | [Date] | [Date] | Payroll Mgr | Completed | Posted to GL same day |
| Employee Delivery | [Date] | [Date] | Payroll Mgr | Completed | All received |

Narrative:
- Overall process adherence (on-time, delays, issues)
- Controls executed as designed
- Any deviations or override procedures used
- Evidence of segregation of duties

### 3. Exception & Error Log (1 page)
Any exceptions, corrections, or variances identified:

| Exception ID | Type | Description | Root Cause | Resolution | Approved By | Resolved Date |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXC-001 | Data | Missing state tax record for 3 employees | System import error | Corrected & re-validated | Controller | [Date] |
| EXC-002 | Manual Adjustment | Bonus payment overstated | Calculation error | Reversal & correction | CFO | [Date] |

Narrative: Overall exception volume (low/medium/high), nature of issues, remediation status, preventive measures.

### 4. Approval Evidence & Authorization Chain (1/2 page)
Document sign-offs:
- HR Director approval of headcount and benefit changes: [Date], [Signature]
- Department Manager approval of payroll: [Date], [Signatures]
- Finance Controller approval of GL posting: [Date], [Signature]
- Executive (CFO/CEO) approval if required: [Date], [Signature]

Narrative: All required approvals obtained? Any gaps or delayed authorizations?

### 5. Data Quality Metrics (1/2 page)
Assess data accuracy and completeness:
| Metric | Value | Target | Status |
|--------|---|---|---|
| Data Completeness | X% | 100% | [Green/Yellow/Red] |
| Data Validation Pass Rate | X% | 100% | [Green/Yellow/Red] |
| Duplicate/Anomaly Detection | X items | 0 | [Green/Yellow/Red] |
| Manual Adjustment Ratio | X% | <2% | [Green/Yellow/Red] |
| Tax Withholding Accuracy | X% | >99% | [Green/Yellow/Red] |

Narrative: Overall data quality assessment. Any findings or areas requiring remediation.

### 6. Compliance Checklist (1/2 page)
Confirm payroll control compliance:

| Control | Expected | Performed | Evidence | Status |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Segregation of Duties (payroll processing) | Yes | Yes | Process flow doc | Compliant |
| Approval of new employees | Yes | Yes | HR file, signatures | Compliant |
| Approval of terminations | Yes | Yes | HR file, signatures | Compliant |
| Tax withholding validation | Yes | Yes | Tax report | Compliant |
| Benefit deduction accuracy | Yes | Yes | Vendor reconciliation | Compliant |
| GL reconciliation | Yes | Yes | GL report | Compliant |

Narrative: Overall control environment assessment. Any gaps or areas of concern.

### 7. Risk Assessment (1/2 page)
Identify any control risks or audit concerns:
- Data integrity risks
- Segregation of duties gaps
- Approval chain weaknesses
- System access or security concerns
- Compliance risks (tax, wage & hour, benefits)
- Trend concerns (anomalies, unusual patterns)

For each: Description | Risk Level (Low/Med/High) | Mitigation | Status

### 8. Supporting Documentation
Attach or reference:
- Payroll data export (summary, not individual employee details)
- Tax withholding calculations
- Benefit reconciliation
- GL posting journal entries
- Vendor invoices for benefits/taxes (summary)
- Manager approval documentation
- System logs/audit trail extracts (if available)

## BPO Client Report Template

**Purpose:** Professional service delivery report demonstrating value and SLA performance to external clients

**Structure:**

### 1. Title Page
```
[CLIENT LOGO / LETTERHEAD]
PAYROLL PROCESSING REPORT
[Client Name]
Period: [Month/Year]
Report Date: [Date]
Prepared by: [BPO Company Name]
```

### 2. Executive Summary (1/2 page)
Professional greeting + high-level status:

"During [Month], we successfully processed payroll for your [X] active employees across [X] pay areas. All deliverables met Service Level Agreements with [X]% on-time accuracy. This report summarizes our work and performance metrics for the period."

Key highlights:
- Headcount processed
- Payment accuracy
- Processing timeliness
- Any client-specific items completed

### 3. Processing Summary (1/2 page)
- **Employees Processed:** Active count
- **Payroll Cycles:** How many pay periods/cycles
- **Total Gross Payroll:** Amount
- **Processing Status:** On-time? Any delays?
- **Exceptions:** How many issues and resolution
- **Client-Specific Requirements:** Any special handling or requests this period

### 4. SLA Scorecard (1/2 page)
Table with client-agreed metrics:

| SLA Metric | Target | Achieved | Status | Notes |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| Processing Accuracy | 99.5% | 99.8% | Pass | Zero material errors |
| On-Time Delivery | 100% | 100% | Pass | Delivered on schedule |
| Tax Filing Accuracy | 99.0% | 100% | Pass | All tax files validated |
| Benefit Reconciliation | 100% | 100% | Pass | All deductions verified |
| Payroll Report Delivery | 2 business days | 1 day | Pass | Ahead of schedule |
| Issue Resolution Time | 24 hours | 4 hours avg | Pass | Fast turnaround |

Overall Status: [Green/Yellow/Red] - "All service levels maintained this period."

### 5. Issue Log & Resolution (1/2 page)
Any issues identified and how they were handled:

| Issue # | Date Reported | Description | Root Cause | Resolution | Resolution Date | Impact |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ISS-001 | [Date] | Employee tax record mismatch | Data entry error | Corrected & reprocessed | [Date] | No late pay |
| ISS-002 | [Date] | Benefit deduction discrepancy | Plan eligibility question | Confirmed with client | [Date] | Deduction applied |

Narrative: Overall issue volume (low/medium/high), responsiveness, and client satisfaction.

### 6. Key Activities Completed (bullet format)
What work was done this period:
- Payroll processing for [X] pay cycles
- Tax withholding calculations and validation
- Benefit deduction processing and reconciliation
- Payroll report generation and delivery
- Employee tax document processing (if applicable)
- Special projects or requests (e.g., "Assisted with benefits enrollment file upload," "Updated tax withholding forms for [X] employees")

### 7. Upcoming Actions & Deadlines (1/2 page)
Proactive communication about next steps:
- Next payroll date and timeline
- Upcoming tax filings or reporting deadlines
- Benefit plan changes or enrollment periods
- System updates or process changes
- Any client action items or requirements

Format as bullet list with dates.

### 8. Contact Information & Support
- Primary contact name, email, phone
- Escalation contact for urgent issues
- Hours of availability
- Portal/system access information (if applicable)

### 9. Appendix: Detailed Tables (reference only)
- Full payroll summary by employee (sensitive—provide securely if needed)
- Department/location breakdown
- Deduction reconciliation detail
- Tax withholding detail
- Payment reconciliation

## General Formatting Guidelines

**All Reports Should Include:**

1. **Professional Header**
   - Company/client logo
   - Report title
   - Period covered
   - Date generated
   - Confidentiality marking

2. **Consistent Styling**
   - Title: 18pt, bold, company color
   - Heading 1: 14pt, bold, dark blue
   - Heading 2: 12pt, bold, dark gray
   - Body: 11pt, black, single-spaced
   - Table header: 11pt, bold, light gray background
   - Table body: 10pt, white background

3. **Margins & Spacing**
   - 1-inch margins on all sides
   - 12pt space after paragraphs
   - 6pt space after tables
   - Page break before major sections

4. **Page Numbers & Footers**
   - Page numbers in footer (Page X of Y)
   - Report period in footer left
   - Confidentiality marking in footer right

5. **Tables**
   - Clear header rows with distinct formatting
   - Alternating row colors (white/light gray) for readability
   - Right-align numbers, left-align text
   - Currency formatted with $ and 2 decimals
   - Percentages with 1-2 decimals

6. **Data Presentation**
   - Summary tables first, detail appendix
   - Narrative before tables explaining what to expect
   - Visual hierarchy: larger numbers, bold for key metrics
   - Use footnotes for explanations or caveats
